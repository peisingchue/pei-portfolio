✦ About Me

The Person
Behind the Products

I'm a Product Manager based in Singapore, originally from Penang — Malaysia's food paradise (yes, I'm spoilt with good food). I crossed the causeway in Sept 2022 as a foreign worker to build my first SG product, and never stopped.

Over 3.5 years at Koomi, I've owned products A–Z — from the first Figma frame to client training, App Store deployment, and live installs across Singapore, JB, and KL. Lately, I've been actively integrating AI into my daily workflow — using Claude and ChatGPT to draft PRDs faster, Figma AI to accelerate design exploration, and AI-assisted tools to write test cases and analyse user feedback.

Before tech, I spent 5+ years in tourism at Amazing Borneo in Sabah — where I learned to sell experiences, lead teams, and get things done across cultures. When I'm not shipping products, I'm underwater. Diving is life.

▲ Real challenge

Migrated 20,000 customer records — points, vouchers, history — from a client's previous platform into Koomi CRM. The migration landed in October with a wave of ad-hoc requests that pushed the scope well beyond the original brief. Not everything was perfect on day one. I stayed on it — adjusting data, communicating gaps honestly with the client, and working through every issue methodically. By January we were revisiting edge cases. By March it was closed. What I learned: a migration is never just data. It's trust, and you earn it back through consistency, not perfection.

CRM Website Integration Ongoing
